## Relay 0.7.0

**Nothing changes for owners.** An existing relay keeps its store, its data folder or Temporal namespace, its pairing and its sign-ins. No step is needed after the update.

New and optional: the relay can keep its state in a backend plugin (the [plugin protocol](https://github.com/spec-studio-app/plugin-sdk/blob/main/PROTOCOL.md)).

- `RELAY_PLUGIN` runs a plugin command that the relay starts; `RELAY_PLUGIN_URL` and `RELAY_PLUGIN_TOKEN` use a plugin sidecar over HTTP.
- `RELAY_PLUGIN_<NAME>` settings go to the plugin.
- At start, the log names the plugin, its version, its repo and its capabilities.
- The Helm chart has `plugin.command` and `plugin.sidecar`; `compose.yaml` has the settings and a sidecar example.

The official plugins, [plugin-json](https://github.com/spec-studio-app/plugin-json) and [plugin-temporal](https://github.com/spec-studio-app/plugin-temporal), come in a later version. Until then, `RELAY_STORE=json` and `RELAY_STORE=temporal` use the built-in stores, as before.
